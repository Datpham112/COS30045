Who made this site and how to read the data behind it.

About this project

This site was made for a data visualisation unit at Swinburne University of Technology. It helps Australian households compare the running costs of televisions using public energy label data.

Where the data comes from

The data is the Australian Government's Energy Rating register of labelled televisions, downloaded from data.gov.au as the file tv_2026_09_28.csv (28 September 2026). Manufacturers and importers report the test results when they register a model.

How it was prepared

The file has 5,340 rows. Superseded entries and models marked unavailable were removed, and 2,157 rows that repeat another row's test result under a different model number were merged. That leaves 2,867 unique test results for TVs currently on sale. Screen sizes were converted from centimetres to inches.

What to keep in mind

Energy figures come from a standard test at 10 hours of use a day, not from real homes. Each model counts once whether it sells thousands or a handful, so the medians describe the range of models on offer, not what people actually buy.
